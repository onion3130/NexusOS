# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

NexusOS is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Assume good intent while discussing technical trade-offs.
- Give and receive feedback professionally.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its community.
- Respect privacy and do not share secrets, personal data, or private reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, trolling, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, and other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns privately through the contact method described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Please do not make public accusations or disclose private information.

This code of conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
